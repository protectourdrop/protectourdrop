The Current Landscape

Malaysia faces a unique paradox: abundant rainfall, yet frequent water disruptions. Our national per-capita domestic water consumption averages well over 200 liters daily—significantly higher than the WHO's recommended 165 liters.

Rapid urbanization, pollution, and climate change are placing unprecedented stress on our river catchments. Preserving these delicate ecosystems is critical for our nation's survival.

Our Vision

A water-secure Malaysia where informed citizens actively protect and preserve aquatic ecosystems.

Our Mission

To eliminate public misconceptions, educate communities on hydrological science, and foster responsible water usage habits.

Advocacy Objectives & Scientific Core

Our approach combines community outreach with foundational environmental science to drive lasting change.

1. Public Literacy

Elevating public understanding of local hydrology, water sourcing, and treatment costs.

2. Habitual Change

Providing actionable, practical steps for households to significantly reduce water footprints.

3. Environmental Stewardship

Empowering citizens to protect local waterways and advocate for sustainable policies.
