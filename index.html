The Current Landscape & Purpose

Crafted as an official assessment deliverable for the course FCS0643 Information Technology, this project confronts a striking Malaysian paradox: abundant annual rainfall juxtaposed with persistent, disruptive water shortages.

With national domestic consumption per-capita exceeding sustainable international metrics, urban expansion and pollution heavily strain our river catchments. By leveraging modern digital tools, we bridge scientific research with public engagement.

Our Vision

To foster a water-secure Malaysia where digitally empowered citizens proactively conserve and protect vital aquatic ecosystems.

Our Mission

To dispel public misconceptions, disseminate vital hydrological insights, and cultivate lifelong resource-saving habits through innovative digital platforms.

Methodology & Framework

Advocacy Objectives & Scientific Core

Our assessment framework combines technical outreach with environmental science to foster enduring behavioral shifts.

1. Public Literacy

Advancing community awareness regarding municipal water sourcing, hydrological processing, and economic costs.

2. Habitual Change

Providing streamlined, actionable techniques enabling households to drastically diminish daily water footprints.

3. Environmental Stewardship

Empowering individuals to protect local watersheds and advocate for progressive, sustainable policy enforcement.
