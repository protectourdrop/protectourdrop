The Current Landscape

Malaysia faces a unique paradox: abundant rainfall, yet frequent water disruptions. Our national per-capita domestic water consumption averages well over 200 liters daily—significantly higher than the WHO's recommended 165 liters.

Rapid urbanization, pollution, and climate change are placing unprecedented stress on our river catchments. Preserving these delicate ecosystems is no longer optional; it is a critical necessity for our nation's survival and prosperity.

Our Vision

A water-secure Malaysia where informed citizens actively protect and preserve aquatic ecosystems.

Our Mission

To eliminate public misconceptions, educate communities on hydrological science, and foster responsible water usage habits through evidence-based outreach.

Advocacy Objectives & Scientific Core

Our approach combines community outreach with foundational environmental science to drive lasting change.

1. Public Literacy

Elevating public understanding of local hydrology, water sourcing, and the unseen costs of water treatment and distribution.

2. Habitual Change

Providing actionable, practical steps for households and businesses to significantly reduce their daily water footprint.

3. Environmental Stewardship

Empowering citizens to protect local waterways, report pollution, and advocate for sustainable policies.

The Science Behind the Drop

  • River Catchment Dynamics

    Forests act as natural sponges. When catchments are deforested, water flows too quickly into rivers, causing flash floods and reducing groundwater recharge.

  • Urban Surface Runoff

    In cities, impermeable surfaces like concrete prevent water absorption. This runoff picks up oil, trash, and chemicals, dumping them directly into our rivers without treatment.

  • Modern Purification Realities

    Treating polluted river water requires immense energy and expensive chemicals. High pollution levels can force treatment plants to shut down entirely, causing widespread cuts.

"Healthy catchments are the foundation of water security."

Community Water Checklist

Actionable steps your household can take today:

  • Check and repair household water taps for leaks.
  • Install water-saving aerators on kitchen and bathroom sinks.
  • Properly dispose of chemical waste—never pour paint or oil down drains.
  • Report illegal dumping near local river catchments to Jabatan Alam Sekitar.
